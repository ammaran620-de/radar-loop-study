Radar Loop Study

Frozen learned radar descriptors propose revisits. Independent scan registration checks their geometric support. Supplied positions evaluate both stages.

Research scope. The learned network is the authors’ pretrained RadarLoc checkpoint. Registration gates are experimental and uncalibrated. Results are a small cross-run study; map optimization, odometry, and embedded deployment remain future work.

Read the evidence

A learned descriptor retrieves three reference observations. A separate hand-engineered descriptor supplies only a coarse angular initialization for registration.

Multiple-start trimmed ICP estimates a 2D transform. Fixed checks examine symmetric return overlap, residual, angular support, and translation magnitude. Ground truth never initializes registration or chooses matches.

A candidate passing these checks is still experimental. Repeated structures and radar multipath can produce false acceptance.

Retrieval recall counts queries that have a nearby reference. Accepted precision counts only queries for which the first passing candidate is selected. Rejected queries are abstentions. The two measures have different denominators.

Protocol and model provenance

Fixed verification settings were chosen as engineering starting points, without label-based tuning. Their precision is measured here, not guaranteed. The sample is too small to establish safety or generalization.
