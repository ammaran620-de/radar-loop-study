How matching works

Strip the 11 metadata bytes from each azimuth row. Boreas byte 10 is unused. Apply the −0.31 m range offset and keep the 2.5–100 m interval. Subtract the scan median and clamp at zero. Average intensity into 60 angular sectors and 32 range rings, then L2-normalize.

Compare descriptors using the maximum cosine score over circular angular shifts. A ring-mean cosine baseline is evaluated separately. Ground-truth coordinates are used only after ranking.

Similarity is a descriptor score, not a probability. The best angular shift is not a calibrated pose estimate.

Protocol and limitations

RadiusEligible queriesRecall@1Recall@5

Recall is computed only for queries with at least one reference within the selected radius. Coverage reports how many queries meet that condition. Top-1 success across all queries is reported separately. No candidate-acceptance threshold has been calibrated; precision of accepted loop closures is not reported.

This is a small exploratory subset, not the official Boreas benchmark. Radius sensitivity is shown to make the evaluation rule visible, not to select the most flattering result. No deskewing, sensor-fusion, geometric verification, or trajectory correction is implemented.
